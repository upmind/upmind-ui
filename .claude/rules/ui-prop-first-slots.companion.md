> Companion to `ui-prop-first-slots` — Upmind-monorepo bindings.

- The composed components are the folder-root mains in `design-system/packages/ui/src/components/<slug>/`. The parts under `parts/` are out of scope.
- This rule is composed-component law CC2. The contract is `design-system/packages/ui/COMPONENT_SPEC.md` and `docs/adr/024-adopt-new-upmind-ui-in-tree.md` §2.
- Exemplar: `design-system/packages/ui/src/components/description-list/DescriptionList.vue`.
