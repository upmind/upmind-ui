## Provenance for "shape without capability counts as a drop"

The concrete archetype is the **FE-2824 client-email pilot**: the staff actor was declared in the scope matrix but never wired — the module "passed" every structural gate while missing the capability. This is the exact failure the base rule's "shape without capability" clause exists to block, and it is the same FE-2824 archetype cited by `reality-check.companion.md` and `negative-controls.companion.md`.
